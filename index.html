How it works

Four steps. Zero effort on your end.

Step 01

Squares get assigned

Our team randomly drops MVP names into a 10×10 board — one axis per team playing that night.

Step 02

Numbers get shuffled

The digits 0–9 are randomly assigned to each row and column. Nobody — including us — picks their own numbers.

Step 03

Quarters lock in scores

At the end of each quarter, we take the last digit of each team's score. That intersection is the winning square.

Step 04

Winners get rewarded

Four scoring moments per game — end of Q1, Q2, Q3, and final — mean four chances to win on every board.

Say it's Chiefs vs. Broncos

The Chiefs sit on the top axis, the Broncos on the side. If the Chiefs lead 10–7 at the end of a quarter, we only care about the last digit of each score.

CHIEFS 10 → 0  ·  BRONCOS 7 → 7

Whoever's sitting in square 0 / 7 wins that quarter. That's it — no skill, no strategy, just being in the right box.
